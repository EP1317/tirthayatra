Significance in mythology

Associated with Goswami Tulsidas, who is said to have received Hanuman’s darshan here. Tuesday/Saturday crowds and the Sankat Mochan music festival keep living arts tied to devotion.

Local and pan-Indian Ramayana trail devotion connects this place to Rama–Sita–Lakshmana geography as remembered in epic and regional telling.

Classical pilgrimage teaching places Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple, Varanasi within India’s tirtha network: a location where devotion to Lord Hanuman is believed to ripen vows more readily than in ordinary space. Epic, Puranic, and regional mahatmya literature — not a single uniform text — sustain this sense of sacred geography.

Local stories & beliefs

In popular pilgrimage memory, Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple, Varanasi is especially associated with Varanasi’s famous Hanuman temple & music tradition. Guides and elders retell this identity to first-time visitors as the living ‘reason’ the tirtha draws crowds.

Local sthala-purana and priestly teaching at Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple, Varanasi elaborate how Lord Hanuman blesses devotees who arrive with vows, gratitude, or grief. Festival days intensify these beliefs through processions, special alankara, and community feeding where practised.

Pilgrims commonly link practical customs — dress, queue, prasadam, and parikrama — with spiritual fruit. Such customs are living belief: they vary by region and should be followed as posted by the temple trust or endowment board.
