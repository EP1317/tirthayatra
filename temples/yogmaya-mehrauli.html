Significance in mythology

Yogmaya Temple, Mehrauli at Mehrauli, New Delhi is dedicated to Goddess Yogmaya, known especially for Phool Walon Ki Sair · historic Delhi Devi search. Pilgrimage tradition places the shrine within India’s tirtha network — a seat where vows, gratitude, and family kuladevi / ishta devotion gather. Local sthala-purana and priestly teaching elaborate how Goddess Yogmaya blesses those who arrive with sincere intent; festival days intensify these beliefs through special alankara and community worship where practised.

Local stories & beliefs

In popular pilgrimage memory, Yogmaya Temple, Mehrauli is especially associated with Phool Walon Ki Sair · historic Delhi Devi search. Guides and elders retell this identity to first-time visitors as the living ‘reason’ the tirtha draws crowds.

Local sthala-purana and priestly teaching at Yogmaya Temple, Mehrauli elaborate how Goddess Yogmaya blesses devotees who arrive with vows, gratitude, or grief. Festival days intensify these beliefs through processions, special alankara, and community feeding where practised.

Pilgrims commonly link practical customs — dress, queue, prasadam, and parikrama — with spiritual fruit. Such customs are living belief: they vary by region and should be followed as posted by the temple trust or endowment board.
