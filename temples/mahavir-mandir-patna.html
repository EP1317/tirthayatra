Significance in mythology

Mahavir Mandir, Patna at Patna Junction area, Patna, Bihar is dedicated to Lord Hanuman, known especially for Patna’s hugely popular Hanuman temple. Pilgrimage tradition places the shrine within India’s tirtha network — a seat where vows, gratitude, and family kuladevi / ishta devotion gather. Local sthala-purana and priestly teaching elaborate how Lord Hanuman blesses those who arrive with sincere intent; festival days intensify these beliefs through special alankara and community worship where practised.

Local and pan-Indian Ramayana trail devotion connects this place to Rama–Sita–Lakshmana geography as remembered in epic and regional telling.

Local stories & beliefs

In popular pilgrimage memory, Mahavir Mandir, Patna is especially associated with Patna’s hugely popular Hanuman temple. Guides and elders retell this identity to first-time visitors as the living ‘reason’ the tirtha draws crowds.

Local sthala-purana and priestly teaching at Mahavir Mandir, Patna elaborate how Lord Hanuman blesses devotees who arrive with vows, gratitude, or grief. Festival days intensify these beliefs through processions, special alankara, and community feeding where practised.

Pilgrims commonly link practical customs — dress, queue, prasadam, and parikrama — with spiritual fruit. Such customs are living belief: they vary by region and should be followed as posted by the temple trust or endowment board.
