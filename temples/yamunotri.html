Yamunotri Temple

India · Uttarkashi district, Uttarakhand

First stop of the Uttarakhand Chota Char Dham — Yamunotri temple near the Yamuna’s sacred source region.

Mythology & Significance

यमुनायै नमः

Salutation to Goddess Yamuna

Devi mantra of the Yamunotri dham

Significance in mythology

Yamunotri is the Devi shrine of the Yamuna in the Uttarakhand Chota Char Dham. Puranic river goddess theology treats Yamuna as sister of Yama and a purifying tirtha; the Himalayan shrine marks the sacred source-region of that devotion.

Pilgrimage literature treats Yamunotri Temple as a tirtha — a crossing where devotion to Goddess Yamuna is believed to bear fruit more readily than in ordinary space. Accounts summarised here follow widely cited epic, Puranic, and regional mahatmya traditions; temple priests remain the guides for ritual detail.

Local stories & beliefs

Pilgrims cook rice in hot springs near the temple as prasadam tradition and regard the tough trek as offering. Seasonal opening and closing frame the goddess’s presence in local calendrical belief.

Yamunotri is consciously distinguished from Adi Shankaracharya’s pan-India Char Dham in informed pilgrimage teaching.

Scriptural & traditional links: Puranic Yamuna mahatmya traditions, Uttarakhand Chota Char Dham pilgrimage lore, Accounts summarised from widely cited Puranic, epic, and pilgrimage traditions; local tellings may differ.

Nearest airport

Local language
Hindi and Garhwali/Kumaoni; English in tourist towns.

Accommodation

Dharamshalas, temple-trust guest houses, and private hotels near Yamunotri Temple. Book early for festival weeks; prefer official trust counters when available.

Local food

Simple hill vegetarian meals and prasadam; carry dry snacks on treks.

Other food options

Wider vegetarian and city cuisine usually in the nearest town; keep non-veg and alcohol away from temple precincts as posted.

Special entry / passes
