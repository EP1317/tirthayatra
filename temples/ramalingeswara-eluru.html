Ramalingeswara Swamy Temple, Eluru

India · Eluru, Andhra Pradesh

Ramalingeswara — central Shiva temple of Eluru town.

शिव जी · Shiva

Mythology & Significance

Significance in mythology

Godavari-belt Shaiva seat; Maha Shivaratri and Kartik Mondays for West Godavari families.

Pilgrimage literature treats this shrine as a living tirtha — verify custom with temple priests.

Queue discipline, prasadam sharing, and festival vows shape belief as practice.
