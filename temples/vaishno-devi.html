Shri Mata Vaishno Devi

India · Trikuta Hills, Katra, Jammu

Vaishno Devi — one of India’s largest Devi pilgrimages, administered by the Shrine Board.

Mythology & Significance

जय माता दी

Victory to the Mother

North Indian pilgrim greeting of the Trikuta yatra

Significance in mythology

Vaishno Devi on Trikuta is worshipped as a form of the Goddess combining Vaishnava and Shakta resonances in North Indian devotion. Regional mahatmya narrates the goddess’s pursuit of the devotee-turned-adversary Bhairon and her establishment in the holy cave — a story that frames the yatra as both refuge and testing.

The shrine board pilgrimage is among India’s largest Devi yatras, placing Trikuta alongside pan-Indian Shakta geography even where formal peetha lists differ.

Local stories & beliefs

The climb from Katra, the holy cave (garbha joon), and the three pindis are the core of belief. Devotees hold that the Mother calls whom she chooses.

Bhairavnath temple visit after the main cave is a customary completion of the yatra in local teaching — gratitude after the Mother’s grace, and closure of the pursuit story told on the trail.

Special entry / passes
