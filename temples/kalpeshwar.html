Kalpeshwar Temple

India · Urgam Valley, Chamoli, Uttarakhand

Fifth Panch Kedar — associated with Shiva’s hair/jata; relatively more accessible than some siblings.

शिव जी · ShivaPanch Kedar

Temple Glimpse

Mythology & Significance

ॐ नमः शिवाय

I bow to Shiva — the auspicious one

Universal Shaiva mantra of this shrine’s living devotion

Significance in mythology

Kalpeshwar completes the Panch Kedar, associated with Shiva’s jata (matted hair) in the Garhwal limb-distribution legend. In the same Pandava–Shiva bull narrative that sanctifies Kedarnath, the locks of Shiva are said to have remained at Kalpeshwar in Urgam — a cave-linked seat of matted, ascetic Shiva.

Puranic Himalayan Shaivism often praises jata as the seat of Ganga’s descent; local teaching quietly echoes that symbolism at this final Kedar.

Local stories & beliefs

Often more accessible than higher Kedars, Kalpeshwar is believed by many to remain approachable longer in the year. Urgam valley lore emphasises a cave-like intimacy with the deity.

Completing all five Kedars is a prized vow among Garhwal and plains pilgrims alike; guides narrate the limb map of Shiva as pilgrims walk from one valley to the next.

Scriptural & traditional links: Panch Kedar sthala tradition, Garhwal Shaiva pilgrimage lore, Accounts summarised from widely cited Puranic, epic, and pilgrimage traditions; local tellings may differ.

Known for: Panch Kedar hair-form; often open longer

Festivals & sacred days

Best time to go
May–June and September–October for high dhams; plains milder October–March.
Climate
Himalayan — cool to cold by altitude; monsoon landslide risk on hill roads.
What to carry
Warm layers and rain protection for hills. Modest clothes, ID proof, water, and offline maps.

Accommodation

Dharamshalas, temple-trust guest houses, and private hotels near Kalpeshwar Temple. Book early for festival weeks; prefer official trust counters when available.

Simple hill vegetarian meals and prasadam; carry dry snacks on treks.

Wider vegetarian and city cuisine usually in the nearest town; keep non-veg and alcohol away from temple precincts as posted.
