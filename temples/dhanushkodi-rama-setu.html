Dhanushkodi & Rama Setu viewpoint

India · Dhanushkodi, Rameswaram tip, Tamil Nadu

Dhanushkodi — the land’s end facing Adam’s Bridge / Rama Setu, climax geography of the Ramayana’s Lanka march.

राम जी · RamaRamayana Trail

Temple Glimpse

Mythology & Significance

Significance in mythology

After Ramanathaswamy darshan, pilgrims ride to Dhanushkodi where the sea opens toward the chain of shoals remembered as Rama Setu. It is among the most searched Ramayana landscape queries worldwide — epic memory meeting a living shoreline.

Classical pilgrimage teaching places this tirtha within India’s epic geography. Epic, Puranic, and regional mahatmya literature — not a single uniform text — sustain this sacred memory.

Local stories & beliefs

In popular pilgrimage memory, Dhanushkodi & Rama Setu viewpoint is especially associated with Rama Setu viewpoint · Setubandha pilgrimage. Guides and elders retell this identity to first-time visitors as the living reason the tirtha draws crowds.

Local sthala-purana and priestly teaching elaborate how the deity blesses devotees who arrive with vows, gratitude, or grief. Festival days intensify these beliefs through processions, special alankara, and community feeding where practised.

Known for: Rama Setu viewpoint · Setubandha pilgrimage
