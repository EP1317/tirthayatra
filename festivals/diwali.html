Diwali / Deepavali

दीपावली

The festival of lights — Rama’s return, Lakshmi’s grace, and homes lit against the dark.

Open festival calendar

  • 2027-10-29 — Diwali / Deepavali
  • 2025-10-20 — Diwali / Deepavali

Meaning

दीपावली संघर्ष के बाद आनंद है — कई परिवार राम–सीता की अयोध्या वापसी याद करते हैं; अन्य लक्ष्मी पूजा से समृद्धि माँगते हैं।

Diwali marks joy after struggle: many families remember Rama and Sita’s homecoming to Ayodhya; others emphasise Lakshmi Puja for prosperity and purity. Across India it closes the autumn festival season with lamps, sweets, and family gatherings.

The story

वनवास और लंका युद्ध के बाद राम–सीता–लक्ष्मण अयोध्या लौटते हैं — नगरी दीपों से स्वागत करती है। समानांतर में अमावस्या लक्ष्मी आगमन की रात; दक्षिण में नरकासुर वध की स्मृति — क्रूरता की हार के बाद प्रकाश।

After years of exile and the war in Lanka, Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana return to Ayodhya. The city lights rows of lamps so the beloved king can find his way home — a people’s festival of welcome. In parallel traditions, the dark Amavasya night is when Lakshmi is invited into clean, honest homes; in Braj and Gujarat, Krishna and cattle stories (Govardhan) neighbour the main night; in the South, Deepavali also remembers Krishna’s victory over Narakasura — light after the defeat of cruelty.

Mythological significance

दीपावली कई विजयों को एक दीप–आकाश में लाती है — राम धर्म, लक्ष्मी कृपा, कृष्ण रक्षा। साझा भाषा: घर शुद्ध करो, दीप जलाओ, अँधेरा हारो।

Diwali gathers multiple sacred victories under one sky of lamps: Rama’s dharma, Lakshmi’s grace, Krishna’s protection, and the Jain remembrance of Mahavira’s nirvana in some communities. The shared grammar is clear — clean the home, light the lamp, defeat inner darkness.

Deity stories

Rama & Sita

Their return teaches that rightful joy is communal — Ayodhya’s lamps are lit by citizens, not only by the palace.

Lakshmi & Ganesha

Lakshmi Puja seeks sustainable prosperity; Ganesha is invited so wealth arrives without obstacles and with wisdom.

Krishna (Narakasura / Govardhan)

Southern Deepavali often recalls Krishna and Satyabhama ending Narakasura’s terror; the next days may honour Govardhan and cattle — protection of the humble.

How it is celebrated

  • Clean and decorate the home; light diyas or lamps at dusk
  • Lakshmi–Ganesha puja in the evening (regional timings vary)
  • Share sweets, new clothes, and greetings with family and neighbours
  • Visit a temple if your community holds Diwali aarti or mela

For the diaspora

भारत के बाहर दीपावली अक्सर तिथि की रात और निकटतम सप्ताहांत दोनों पर मनाई जाती है। मंदिर लक्ष्मी पूजा और सांस्कृतिक कार्यक्रम रखते हैं।

Outside India, Diwali is often celebrated on the nearest weekend as well as the tithi night. Hindu temples in the US, UK, Canada, Gulf, Australia, and SE Asia host Lakshmi puja, cultural programmes, and community dinners.

  • United States & Canada: Temple Diwali nights, school/workplace celebrations, weekend melas.
  • United Kingdom & Europe: Community halls and mandirs; fireworks rules vary by city.
  • Gulf & SE Asia: Large temple gatherings; check public holiday calendars.
  • Australia & New Zealand: Spring-season Diwali fairs; evening temple aarti.
