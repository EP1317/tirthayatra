Gudi Padwa

गुड़ी पड़वा

Marathi New Year — raising the gudi for victory, harvest, and Rama’s Ayodhya return memory.

Open festival calendar

Listed dates

  • 2027-04-07 — Gudi Padwa
  • 2026-03-19 — Gudi Padwa

Meaning

गुड़ी पड़वा चैत्र शुक्ल प्रतिपदा पर मराठी–कोंकणी नववर्ष है। रंगीन वस्त्र, तोरण और उल्टे कलश वाली गुड़ी विजय और समृद्धि का निशान है।

Gudi Padwa (also Ugadi in parts of the Deccan/South under related new-year customs) marks Chaitra Shukla Pratipada — the traditional new year for many Maharashtrian and Konkani households. A gudi (bright cloth, garland, and inverted pot on a pole) is raised as a sign of victory and invitation to prosperity.

The story

लोक कथाएँ इसे राम की अयोध्या ओर यात्रा और शालिवाहन/चैत्र नववर्ष की फसल शुरुआत से जोड़ती हैं। गुड़ी अशुभ दूर कर नये वर्ष की शक्ति आमंत्रित करती है।

Folk and regional tellings connect the day with Rama’s return toward Ayodhya and with the start of a blessed agricultural cycle under Shalivahana / Chaitra reckoning. The raised gudi is said to ward off inauspiciousness and announce that the household welcomes the new year’s shakti.

Mythological significance

गुड़ी पड़वा चैत्र नवरात्रि और राम नवमी के निकट है — वर्षारंभ में देवी शक्ति और राम धर्म दोनों का स्पर्श।

As a new-year threshold, Gudi Padwa sits near Chaitra Navaratri and Ram Navami in the calendar — Devi’s spring cycle and Rama’s birth festival follow close, weaving royal dharma and goddess energy into the year’s opening.

Deity stories

Rama

Remembering Rama’s homecoming frames the new year as a return to order, family, and righteous joy after exile’s long road.

Devi (Chaitra cycle)

Because Chaitra Navaratri begins around this period, many homes also keep Devi’s spring welcome in mind while raising the gudi.

How it is celebrated

  • Clean the house; draw rangoli; raise the gudi at the entrance
  • Prepare puran poli / shrikhand and festive Maharashtrian dishes
  • Wear new clothes; visit elders and temples
  • Listen to the year’s panchang reading where the custom exists

For the diaspora

विदेश की मराठी संस्थाएँ गुड़ी पड़वा कार्यक्रम रखती हैं। बालकनी में छोटी गुड़ी पर्याप्त; मंदिर विशेष आरती कर सकते हैं।

Maharashtrian associations abroad host Gudi Padwa brunches and cultural programmes. A small balcony gudi works in apartments; temples may hold special aarti.

  • Maharashtra & Goa: Core Gudi Padwa observance.
  • Deccan diaspora: Often celebrated alongside Ugadi greetings among friends.
  • Global: Community halls; temple new-year aarti.
