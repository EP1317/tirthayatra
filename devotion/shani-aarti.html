शनि आरती — जय जय श्री शनि देव

Shani Aarti — Jai Jai Shri Shani Deva

Today’s practice

Deity
Shiva
Tradition / author
Traditional
When to recite
Shani Amavasya, Saturdays, Shani Shingnapur/Shani temples

Traditional Shani Dev aarti — justice, patience, and graha shanti.

Text note: Traditional hymn / katha presented for personal learning and home puja. Authors and lineages remain with their traditions; TirthaYatra does not claim copyright over classical verses. Optional YouTube listens belong to their uploaders.

पाठ · Text

जय जय श्री शनि देवा, स्वामी जय श्री शनि देवा।
नीलांजन समाभासं, रवि पुत्र नमोस्तुते॥

काल के देवता, न्याय के धाम।
दण्ड देते धीरे, कर्म फल स्वाम॥
जय जय श्री शनि देवा॥

शनि शिंगणापुर, शनि मंदिर में।
भक्त जन प्रार्थना, शांति मन में॥
जय जय श्री शनि देवा॥

ॐ शं शनैश्चराय नमः॥
जय जय श्री शनि देवा॥

Meaning · अर्थ

Shani as slow, just Saturn — not to fear but to respect through honest work and Saturday lamp.
