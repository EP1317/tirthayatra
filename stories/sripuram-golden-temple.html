Lakshmi Narayani tak ka star path

The star path to Lakshmi Narayani

Sone ka path — par chalne wale se dhyaan maangta hai, keval tamasha nahi।

Gold underfoot — but the walk asks for attention, not spectacle alone.

कथा · The story

Sripuram ki Lakshmi Narayani mandir bhakton se star-path par chal kar karuna ke sandesh padhne ko kehti hai, phir golden garbhagriha। Sona camera khinchta hai, par buzurg kahte hain asli arpan bina jaldi ki chaal hai — har kadam ek chinta chhodne ka mauka। Annadanam campus par Devi ke poshan ko bhojan mein failata hai।

Sripuram's Lakshmi Narayani temple asks devotees to walk a star-shaped path reading messages of compassion before reaching the golden sanctum. The gold cladding catches cameras, but elders say the real offering is the unhurried walk — each step a chance to loosen one grip on anxiety. Annadanam at the campus extends the Goddess's nourishment beyond the icon into shared meal.
