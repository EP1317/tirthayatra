WhatsApp share (built into pages)

On every aarti, chalisa, vrat katha, story, and festival guide, use WhatsApp / Copy link under the title. Prefers Hindi titles for Sawan and Chalisa pages so family chats feel native.

Google Search Console (do this once per property)

Add property for https://www.tirthayatraonline.in (Domain or URL-prefix).

Sitemaps → submit https://www.tirthayatraonline.in/sitemap.xml (index of temples, devotion, festivals, stories, images).

Optional: also submit /sitemap-all.xml if the index is slow to expand.

Coverage → fix 404s / redirected URLs; watch queries like सावन सोमवार व्रत कथा, कांवड़ यात्रा, लिंगाष्टकम्, [temple] aarti.

After big content releases (Sawan pack), request indexing on 5–10 hero URLs from the URL Inspection tool.

Language tip

Use the हिंदी | EN toggle on guides and stories. Sawan, Kanwar, Chalisa, and Shiva pages default Hindi-first for first-time visitors from Hindi search.
